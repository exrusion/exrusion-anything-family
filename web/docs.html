Anything documentation

Launch with clarity.

Everything you need to prepare, review and sign launches across six independent launchpads from one workspace.

01

Single launch

Choose one launchpad, upload the token image, enter the token details and configure that provider’s options. Review the route and approve the final transaction in the correct wallet.

02

Multi-launch

Select any 1–6 launchpads and enter the shared token profile once. Anything prepares every selected route and guides you through the required wallet approvals. Each chain confirms independently.

05

Fees and custody

Each provider keeps its own launch and trading fees. Network gas or rent still applies. Anything adds 0% and does not hold launch proceeds, wallet keys or signed transactions.

06

Before signing

Use a square PNG, JPEG or WebP under 2 MB. Check the token name, ticker, pair, taxes, initial buy and recipient addresses. Onchain launches cannot be reversed after confirmation.

07

After launch

Successful launches are saved in the Launched section on this device with their provider, network and explorer link when available. A failed route can be retried without repeating completed launches.
